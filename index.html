What property managers ask us

What does the free audit check?

One thing per door: whether your company is named as an additional insured on the owner's policy. It doesn't review whether the owner has the right coverage.

Won't this annoy our owners?

No. We send owners a short, friendly note from PolicyRoster explaining that we work with you, and they get a free review of their policy and pricing out of it. You approve the note before anything goes out.

What do you need from us?

Two things: your owner list (an export from your property management software works) and the insurance documents you already have on file, such as declarations pages, endorsements or certificates. We don't contact any owner until we have both and you've approved it.

What do our owners have to do?

Sign a one-page authorization online, which takes about two minutes. After that, we deal with their agent directly.

What counts as "verified"?

An endorsement or declarations page from the carrier or agent naming your company. A certificate alone doesn't count.

How does monitoring work?

Cancellation notices are routed to us, and we re-confirm every policy before renewal. If something changes, you hear from us the same day.

Are you an insurance agency?

No. We verify status and handle the paperwork with each owner's authorization. The owner policy review comes from our licensed partner agency, and owners never have to switch.
